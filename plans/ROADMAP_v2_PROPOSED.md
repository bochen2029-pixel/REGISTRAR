## 2 · F-RESIDENT-VS-FLOOR — the fourth falsifier `[BET · designed, unbuilt]`

weekly cron. *Kill condition: if the deterministic closure catches nearly everything the trained judge
would, the resident is theatre and the funeral prints.*
