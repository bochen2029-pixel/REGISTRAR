## PART III · The batteries-included revision `[DOCTRINE CHANGE — PROPOSED, NOT ADOPTED]`

Prompted by the operator showing `C:\scriptorium` (surveyed: MIT licence · S0 green, honest rungs that
refuse · local sidecars for pixels/audio · one metered provider seam for text · a deterministic span-check
fence that caught **28.5% paraphrase-as-quote** in live use · 1,954 files).

### The concession

**"Your tools are as good as ours" is true as a contract and fantasy as a prediction.** An OPO IT shop has
Epic analysts and a ticket queue; a hash-chained, charter-frozen corpus reader will never occur to them *as
a category*. Refusing to ship a default does not preserve their autonomy — it delegates the choice to
whichever dsh plugin trends that month. **A principle that only produces worse defaults is a pose.**

And our own data concedes the second half: **the v4 COVERS was won at toy scale.** `site_v4` fits in a
context window; a real site is the binder plus six years of tickets — 10⁷–10⁸ tokens, exactly the regime
where chunk-and-read-in-order stops being a plan. **The completability thesis is untested at the scale where
`read`/`reach`-class tooling is the difference.** Batteries-included may be load-bearing for R6, not a
convenience.

### What survives of the old doctrine

- **Never vendored** — but the chassis already solved this: **pin, don't vendor.** `SCRIPTORIUM.pin.json`
  on the chassis pattern (fetch, byte-verify, mount through the contract) dissolves licence, drift, and
  redistribution in one move.
- **The rules still refuse the mount.** `local_leg_only` stands absolutely: scriptorium's sovereignty split
  protects pixels, but canonical text rents the reading, and site SOP text is PHI-adjacent. Site corpora go
  through a local leg or they do not go.
- **The contract stays the interface** — scriptorium becomes the *reference binding* for `read`, swappable
  by construction, which keeps the old sentence true while shipping a default for the fifty-four sites that
  have nothing.

### The four shelves (the delineation, settled)

1. **The seed** — law. No plugins, ever.
2. **The forge contract** — capabilities + refuses-the-mount rules. Part of the fence. Ours.
3. **Reference bindings** — pinned external repos (`scriptorium → read`, `cortex → reach`), fetched like
   the chassis, mounted through the contract, honest rungs and all. `forge/boot.py --with read` is the
   natural continuation of F-BOOT.
4. **The ecosystem** — everyone else's dsh plugins, which will exist regardless. **Our role is the customs
   office, not the monopoly**: any plugin, ours or theirs, mounts only through the same conformance rules.
   We do not ship the only tools; we ship the inspection.

### The immediate wedge — zero PHI, already on the roadmap

Scriptorium's first REGISTRAR job should be **public law, not site material**: the ~50 jurisdiction statutes
(currently parked as "theirs, not ours"), the OPTN policy cycle that staleness-decays in months, the Tier
2/3 proceedings from January. A purely public, genuinely huge reading job — the authorization-law ontology
induced under a ratified charter, every claim span-checked — **the jurisdiction table built the way the
thesis says things get built, colliding with nothing in AGENTS.md §3**, and shaking down the binding on real
bulk before any site corpus exists.

## PART IV · Vision and risks, stated plainly

- **The synthetic ceiling is reached** for the *current* corpus scale; F-SCALE is the one synthetic
  experiment left that changes the picture. After it, the next brick is a handshake, not a commit.
- **The pilot protocol** should be drafted before it is needed: shadow mode, read-only, PHI never leaves,
  deliverables = the site's own measures + a fit draft. Writing it forces every implicit promise explicit.
- **The site question that needs a human decision:** the obvious first site is the operator's employer, and
  that is a counsel-and-disclosure conversation, not an engineering one. The clean-room rule bars
  *recollection* as design input; a *sanctioned pilot* is a different animal — but only a human can walk
  that path. Named here so it is not routed around.
- **Monoculture risk of reference bindings:** a shipped default becomes a de-facto standard. Mitigation is
  the customs-office stance plus the swap being genuinely one line — and F-SCALE measuring whether the
  default earns its place.
- **The standing cultural claim, worth protecting above any feature:** three fresh sessions, three
  configurations, one deliberately unanswerable trap, **zero invented values ever.** Every future run
  appends to that series; the first fabrication is a headline event and must be treated as one.
