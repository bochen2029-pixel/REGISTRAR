# SOP-03 · Referral Intake · rev 7 (2026-02)
*FICTIONAL — see README.md. Zero PHI.*
Business hours are 07:00–19:00; the `after_hours` flag in the intake system is set from the arrival clock.
**After-hours referrals route to the House Coordinator** (rev 7 change; formerly the on-call supervisor).
Where first contact does not reach a decision-maker, a second attempt is made **within thirty (30) minutes**.
A referral with no progression for **two hours (120 minutes)** is reviewed at handover.
Timely referral is defined **per participating-hospital agreement** — see contracts/agreements/.
