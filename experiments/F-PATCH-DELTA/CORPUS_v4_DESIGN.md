# Corpus v4 — design, pre-registered before a line of it is built

**`2026-08-27`. The v1 corpus and its three runs stand as recorded; nothing is edited retroactively. v4 is
a NEW corpus under a new seed, and this document is written before the builder.**

## The measured justification — and the line v4 must not cross

Every honest candidate held ~8 answerable targets because the material gave them **no replay surface**, and
the contract (correctly) refuses a row whose shadow run would be invented. So the **maximum honest score was
`23/40 = 0.575`** — below the COVERS band (0.70) **by construction**. The floor "beat" honest candidates
only by answering everything and being gate-refused.

**v4's purpose is to make the full 40 points reachable by honest play — not to make the kit look better.**
The falsifier's verdict on v1 stands published. The test of whether v4 crossed the line: **the floor arm is
regenerated mechanically and must still be gate-refused, and the traps below must still bite.**

## Design rules

1. **Every answerable target gets a replay surface.** New tape files: `ruleouts.csv` (who/criterion/hour —
   plus the Appendix A document), `management.csv` (targets used per case), `escalations.csv` (levels and
   ack minutes), `esign.csv` (capture method and timestamps), `dispatch.csv` (door-to-door derivable),
   `imaging_requests.csv` (route per hospital) — and **two of the hospital agreements as documents**, so
   `timely_referral_definition` is answerable per-hospital and replayable against referral timestamps.
2. **Two designed declines, not one.** `transport.perfusion` stays unanswerable (nothing anywhere).
   `allocation.credentials` becomes the second trap of a *different* kind: the mechanism is documented, the
   secret is not — the correct output is a row for the mechanism **and** a `phi_adjacent_declined` hold for
   the rest. Tests a split judgment, not just absence.
3. **The planted contradictions stay, in both directions** — binder stricter than tape, tape stricter than
   binder, and org-chart-vs-practice — on *different* targets than v1, so v1 knowledge does not transfer.
4. **The v1 corpus's three accidental defects become v4 construction invariants**, checked by the builder:
   the case tables must reconcile as one funnel (referral ⊇ workup ⊇ allocation ⊇ recovery, authorization
   consistent); `after_hours` must derive from the clock per the SOP; every volume claim in a document must
   match the tape it summarises. **The builder refuses to emit a corpus that violates its own invariants** —
   the same law-9 move as everywhere else.
5. **Scoring: rubric v3 unchanged, bands unchanged** (0.70 / 0.40 + exceeds-the-floor + abstention ≥ 8).
   The delta is extracted after the corpus is written, per the original §3 discipline. Honest ceiling under
   v4 = 40/40, so the bands finally mean what they say.
6. **Roles:** the corpus author (mainline) is already disqualified as runner. The arm-② runner is a fresh
   session per the ledger; the floor arm is regenerated mechanically from the same prior list as v1, no new
   priors added (adding priors after seeing v1's misses would be fitting the floor).

## What v4 decides

If an honest fresh candidate clears SHAPED against the same-rubric floor on a corpus where honesty can
reach 40 — the completability bet survives its falsifier for the first time with meaning. If it still
fails, the kit — not the instrument, not the corpus — is the deficit, and the pre-committed consequence
already published (spine + human guide) becomes the roadmap rather than the fallback.

*Design frozen before the builder exists. Where this and a dated receipt disagree, the receipt wins.*
