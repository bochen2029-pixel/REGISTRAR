# Organ Transport — summary of arrangements

**Fairbank Donor Network · current as of 2025-09**

*FICTIONAL — see [`../README.md`](../README.md).*

---

## Ground

**Meridian Medical Courier** — primary ground provider for destinations within 180 road miles. Dispatch by
telephone, 24/7. Contracted response to pickup: **45 minutes.**

## Air — fixed wing

**Northline Air Charter** — fixed-wing charter beyond ground range. Contracted **wheels-up within 90
minutes** of a confirmed request, subject to crew duty limits and weather.

## Air — rotor

**No standing rotor-wing arrangement.** Rotor is arranged case by case through the accepting centre where
they have one.

## Commercial

Commercial counter-to-counter is used for **tissue only. Not used for organs.**

---

*Door-to-door figures are not specified in any of these agreements. Coordinators use the dispatch log.*
