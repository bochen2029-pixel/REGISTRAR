## Schedule B — Service Levels

### B.1 Standard serology panel

The Laboratory shall report results for the standard serology panel **within four (4) hours of specimen
receipt**, twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week.

### B.2 Blood typing

Blood typing shall be reported within two (2) hours of specimen receipt. The Network is responsible for
submitting two specimens drawn on separate occasions in accordance with its own protocol.

### B.3 Reporting

Results are delivered to the interface specified in Schedule C. Where the interface is unavailable, results
are telephoned to the Network's clinical line and the written report follows.

### B.4 Escalation

Failure to meet a service level in Schedule B is escalated to the Laboratory Director. Persistent failure is
grounds for review at either party's request.

---

## Schedule C — Interface

Results are delivered by HL7 v2.5.1 ORU^R01 over MLLP to the endpoint designated by the Network.
