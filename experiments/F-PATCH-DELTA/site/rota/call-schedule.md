## Coverage

| Role | Hours | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical Services Coordinator | 07:00–19:00, on site | 4 |
| House Coordinator | 19:00–07:00, **on site** | 2 |
| On-call Supervisor | 24/7, **remote**, one-week rotation | 1 of 3 |
| On-call Physician | 24/7, remote, one-week rotation | 1 of 4 |
| Medical Director | business hours; available for escalation | 1 |

## Notes

The **House Coordinator** role was introduced in 2023 to provide overnight on-site presence. Before that,
overnight coverage was the on-call supervisor only.

The on-call supervisor covers a full week including weekends, and is frequently engaged with an active donor
for extended periods.

Recovery teams are **in-house**. Two coordinators are trained for recovery and one is rostered per shift.
Contracted recovery is used only when both are committed.
