## 18.1 First-person authorization

Where the donor is registered, first-person authorization is verified against the state registry before any
approach to family. The verification is documented in the donor record.

## 18.2 Approach

Approaches are conducted in person wherever the family is present at the hospital. Telephonic approach is
used only where the family is unable to attend and a delay would jeopardise the donation opportunity.

The approach is conducted by a Family Services Coordinator. The referring clinician is invited to be present
for the introduction where the family has an established relationship with them.

## 18.3 Second requester

**A second requester is present for approaches involving a donor under the age of eighteen.**

## 18.4 Documentation

The signed authorization is captured electronically and stored in the donor record. See
[`../interfaces/inventory.md`](../interfaces/inventory.md) for the current capture arrangement.

## 18.5 Declination

A declination is documented with the class of the authorising party and the point in the conversation at
which the decision was reached. No further approach is made.
