# SOP-11 · Triage and Ruleout

**Fairbank Donor Network · Clinical · rev 3 · effective 2025-03-10 · owner: Medical Director**

*FICTIONAL — see [`../README.md`](../README.md).*

---

## 11.1 Initial screen

Every referral receives an initial clinical screen against current ruleout criteria before any further
activity. The screen is performed by a Clinical Services Coordinator.

## 11.2 Authority to rule out

A Clinical Services Coordinator may rule out a referral without escalation where the ruleout is on a
criterion listed in Appendix A (absolute contraindications).

**Any ruleout on clinical judgment rather than a listed criterion requires the Medical Director or the
on-call physician.** This applies at all hours.

## 11.3 Ruleout documentation

Every ruleout records the specific criterion, or the physician who authorised it. A ruleout with neither is
returned for completion.

## 11.4 Callback

Where the initial screen cannot be completed because the referring clinician is unavailable, see SOP-03 §3.4.

## 11.5 Progression

A referral that clears the initial screen proceeds to full evaluation. Cases that do not progress are
documented per SOP-03 §3.6 with the reason recorded.

---

*Appendix A (absolute contraindications) is maintained separately by the Medical Director and reviewed
annually.*
