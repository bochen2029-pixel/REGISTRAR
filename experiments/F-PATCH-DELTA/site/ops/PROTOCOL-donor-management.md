# Donor Management Protocol — summary

**Fairbank Donor Network · Medical Director standing orders · rev 2 · effective 2025-02-01**

*FICTIONAL — see [`../README.md`](../README.md).*

---

## Applicability

These standing orders apply to all deceased donors under Network management, from authorization until
cross-clamp.

## Targets

**Standard adult targets are used. The Network does not maintain a separate protocol variant.**

Deviation from standard targets requires the on-call physician and is documented in the donor record with
the reason.

## Escalation

Physiologic management escalates **directly to the on-call physician** per SOP-24 §24.3. Operational
escalation does not apply to clinical decisions.

## Documentation

Management interventions, and the measurements they were made against, are recorded in the donor record.
