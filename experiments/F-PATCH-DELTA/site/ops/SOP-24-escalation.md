# SOP-24 · Escalation and On-Call

**Fairbank Donor Network · Operations · rev 2 · effective 2024-08-01**

*FICTIONAL — see [`../README.md`](../README.md).*

---

## 24.1 The ladder

1. Clinical Services Coordinator (case owner)
2. House Coordinator
3. On-call Supervisor
4. Director of Clinical Operations
5. Medical Director — **clinical matters only**

## 24.2 Timing

An escalation not acknowledged **within twenty minutes** is escalated to the next level by the originator.

## 24.3 Donor management

Physiologic management decisions escalate **directly to the Medical Director or the on-call physician**,
bypassing operational levels. Operational escalation does not apply to clinical decisions.
