# SOP-03 · Referral Intake

**Fairbank Donor Network · Operations · rev 6 · effective 2024-11-01 · owner: Director of Clinical Operations**

*FICTIONAL — see [`../README.md`](../README.md).*

---

## 3.1 Scope

All referrals received from participating hospitals, by any channel, at any hour.

## 3.2 Receipt

Referrals arrive by the interfaced feed where a hospital has one, and by the referral line where it does
not. Hospital H-1102 and H-1207 are interfaced. H-1355 and H-1490 refer by telephone.

The referral line is answered by the Clinical Services Coordinator during business hours (07:00–19:00).

## 3.3 After-hours receipt

**Outside business hours, referrals route to the on-call supervisor**, who is responsible for initial screen
and for assigning the case.

Where the on-call supervisor is engaged with an active donor, the referral is held in queue and worked at
the earliest opportunity.

## 3.4 First contact

The coordinator taking the referral will attempt contact with the referring clinician to complete the
initial screen.

**Where the first contact does not reach a decision-maker, a second attempt shall be made within thirty (30)
minutes.**

## 3.5 Timely referral

Each participating hospital agreement defines "timely referral" for that hospital. See
[`../contracts/`](../contracts/) and the individual agreements. Coordinators are expected to be familiar
with the definition applicable to the referring hospital.

## 3.6 Documentation

All referrals are documented in the donor record whether or not they progress, including referrals ruled out
on first screen and referrals that do not progress for any other reason.

## 3.7 Escalation

Conflicts regarding donor suitability, family circumstances, or hospital cooperation escalate to the
Director of Clinical Operations. Out of hours, escalation is via the on-call supervisor.
